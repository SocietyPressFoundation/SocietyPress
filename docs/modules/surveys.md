# Surveys

Ask your members questions and see everyone's answers added up. You write the questions and press Publish. SocietyPress makes the page and gives you a link to send out. Each member can answer once, and a survey can be anonymous, so you see who took part but not who said what.

## How to run your first survey

**SocietyPress → Surveys → Add New Survey.**

1. **Survey title.** Members see this at the top (e.g. "2027 Membership Survey").
2. **Introduction.** Optional. Why you're asking, and how long it takes.
3. **Questions.** Type each question and pick the kind of answer:
   - **Multiple choice (pick one)** or **Drop down menu** for things like age group or county.
   - **Checkboxes (pick many)** for "which of these interest you?"
   - **Rating scale (agree/disagree)** comes worded Strongly disagree through Strongly agree. Type your own wording in the choices box if you want something different.
   - **Star rating (1 to 5)** also reports an average.
   - **Paragraph text** for written answers.

   Tick **Required** if everyone must answer it. Use the arrows to change the order.
4. Press **Publish**. The box on the right shows the link. Press **Copy** and paste it into your email or newsletter.

## Anonymous or named

**Anonymous** is on for every new survey.

- **On:** the Results tab lists who has taken part, but no answer is tied to a name. Answers are dated by day only, and the list of who took part has no dates, so nobody can match an answer to a person by when it came in.
- **Off:** each member's name and email appear next to their answers in the spreadsheet. Use this when you need to know who said what, such as "who's bringing what to the potluck."

Members see a line above the questions saying which kind of survey it is.

Once anyone has answered, the Anonymous setting is locked. Members answered under that promise, so it can't be changed afterwards.

## Who can answer

**Members in good standing** is the default. Members sign in, and each one can answer once.

**Anyone who has the link** opens the survey to the public. With no sign-in to check, SocietyPress stops casual repeat answers from the same browser, but someone determined could answer twice. Keep it to members when the count matters.

## Reading the results

Open the survey and choose the **Results** tab. You'll see:

- how many members have answered, out of how many could
- **Who has answered**: click it to see the list
- every question with its answers counted, including choices nobody picked
- every written answer
- **Download answers as a spreadsheet** for your own analysis (anonymous surveys are shuffled, so row order says nothing about who answered first)

## Closing and reopening

**Close Survey** stops new answers. The page stays up and tells latecomers the survey has ended, so the link in your email doesn't lead to an error. Results stay on the Results tab.

**Reopen Survey** brings it back at the same link.

You can change what members see in each case under **Wording**: the button text, the thank-you message, the message for someone who has already answered, and the message after it closes.

## If something looks wrong

**A member says the survey won't let them answer.** They probably aren't signed in, or their membership has lapsed. The survey page tells them which.

**A member says it told them they'd already answered.** They have. Each member gets one answer.

**The survey's page is gone.** If someone deleted it from the Pages screen, open the survey and press **Put the Page Back**.

**Surveys isn't in the menu.** Settings → Modules → turn on Surveys.
